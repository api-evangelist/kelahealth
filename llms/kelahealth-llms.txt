# kelaHealth

> kelaHealth is a healthcare AI company that improves surgical outcomes with a Surgical Intelligence Service — an AI-enabled API delivering patient-specific predictive insights at critical decision points across the perioperative process. Its models, trained on millions of surgical procedures, predict individual patient risk across nine major complication categories, identify modifiable factors, and recommend stratified interventions, embedded into partner medtech and healthcare software applications. kelaHealth is HIPAA compliant and backed by Techstars.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public kelaHealth website. kelaHealth does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or SDKs; its Surgical Intelligence API is delivered through partner integrations rather than a self-serve developer surface.
